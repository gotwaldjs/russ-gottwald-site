The insight

This didn’t just require consideration of their brand value and development process from beginning to end; it meant understanding exactly what “inclusivity” meant in specific regional and cultural contexts – an approach based solely on American understandings and perspectives would go nowhere.

The answer

Our playbook didn’t just explain how inclusivity worked; we seamlessly integrated it within each step of Shure’s process.
